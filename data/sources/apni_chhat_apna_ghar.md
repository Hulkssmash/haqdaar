Apni Chhat Apna Ghar Program (Government of Punjab) has two tracks: "Apni Chhat Apna Ghar" (apply for construction of a new house) and "Apni Chhat Mehfooz Chhat" (apply for addition of a new floor or room). The helpline is 0800-09100 (toll free). Official website: acag.punjab.gov.pk.

Eligibility criteria that apply to both tracks: the applicant should be a Punjab resident (CNIC holder). The applicant should be 21 to 60 years of age at the time of submission of the application. The applicant must have no criminal record. The applicant must not be a loan defaulter.

Eligibility criteria for Apni Chhat Apna Ghar (new house construction): the applicant must be the sole owner and possessor of a parcel of land measuring up to 5 Marla in urban areas or up to 10 Marla in rural areas.

Eligibility criteria for Apni Chhat Mehfooz Chhat (addition of a new floor or room): the applicant must be registered with the National Socioeconomic Registry (NSER/PSER) with a PMT score of 60 or less. The applicant must be the owner of a constructed house up to 5 Marla (urban) or 10 Marla (rural).
